About the Host

Matthew Rascoff

Matthew Rascoff is an education innovation leader who has worked across sectors to democratize access to knowledge and opportunity. He joined Stanford University in 2021 as the inaugural Vice Provost for Digital Education, advising Stanford’s president and provost on digital learning initiatives and establishing Stanford Digital Education (SDE).
